# InnerGames: what they offer (context for the prototype)

Made from innergames.nl on 4 November 2026: 21 products in the shop (9 workshops, 6 games, 1 train-de-trainer, 5 projects) plus the team, contact, home and subsidies pages.

How to use this file:
- **Facts** (prices, durations, group sizes, audience, what's in the box, who made it) are copied exactly from the live site. They are the source of truth. If `src/data.ts` says something different, tell Damian instead of picking one.
- **Descriptions** are short summaries in my own words, so you know what each thing is about. Write the actual website copy in Dutch, in the brand tone. Don't copy the old texts one-to-one; they are often long and only list facts (that was a finding of the benchmark).
- Want the original texts word for word? Run `npm run content`. That saves them in `docs/INNERGAMES_CONTENT_FULL.md`.

Things that apply to every workshop on the site:
- Standard location: the InnerGames office, Kronehoefstraat 87, Eindhoven. Other locations are possible; the price excludes travel costs.
- Prices are per group unless it says "per persoon".
- Booking now: you buy the workshop in the shop and then send your preferred date. Most clients contact Ariën first.

## Workshops (9)

### Energiek team!
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/workshop-energiek-team/
- price: € 2.850 (price per 12 participants)
- duration: about 3 × 4 hours (three dagdelen)
- audience: colleagues in a team
- themes: personal development, resilience, growth, teamwork
- method: The Elemental Five
- what it is: a team programme in three parts. First everyone discovers their own behaviour, habits and what they would rather do. Then the team looks at what drives it together and picks a direction that fits the organisation and the people. In the last part the team makes it practical: dividing workplaces, activities, roles and tasks so people use each other's strengths and get energy from working together.

### Ontdek jouw drijfveren
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/ontdek-jouw-drijfveren/
- price: € 950 (price per 12 participants)
- duration: about 4 hours (a morning or an afternoon), small groups
- themes: personal development, resilience, growth
- method: The Elemental Five (the site calls it "The elemental game" here)
- what it is: the single-session version for individuals: discover your usual behaviour and strategies, what you would rather do, and how to make that happen in daily life. Playful and full of insight.

### Het spel tussen hoofd en lichaam
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/het-spel-tussen-hoofd-en-lichaam/
- price: € 950 (price per 12 participants, 1 trainer)
- duration: about 4 hours. A whole day = buy two dagdelen. Extra days possible in consultation.
- led by: **Wies van Beek & Ariën Kingma** (named on the page)
- themes: personal development, resilience, growth. Mental, physical and emotional balance.
- background on the page: stress and burn-out among Dutch employees (it cites a TNO factsheet: 1 in 6 employees has burn-out complaints, stress is the number one work-related illness).
- what it is: three parts. The Elemental Five as the main thread (what gives you energy, without judgement), body-focused exercises, and breathing exercises to calm the head. Promised results: stronger personal leadership and cooperation, more focus, more resilience and energy, quick stress reduction, more body awareness, balance between head and body.

### De Klimaat Collage
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/de-klimaat-collage-tijdelijk-gratis-demo/ (the slug says "tijdelijk gratis demo", the page itself shows € 1.800)
- price: € 1.800. Ask about discounts for education and NGOs.
- duration: about 3 hours
- group: max 30
- audience: adults, pupils, students
- themes: sustainability, climate change, consumption
- what it is: an interactive workshop where the group maps how the parts of the climate problem connect, using 42 information cards. Talking, drawing, brainstorming and writing, ending with solutions. Can be led by a workshop leader or a teacher.

### Het Impact Imperium
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/het-impact-imperium/
- price: € 423,50
- duration: 1,5 to 2 hours
- group: max 32
- audience: pupils and students
- themes: sustainability, consumption, circular economy
- made by: Duurzaamheid in de Klas (student workshops that combine serious gaming and creative design)

### Hoezo Raar?!

### Positive Masculinity
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/workshop-positive-masculinity/
- price: € 350
- duration: about 2 hours

- themes: masculinity, vulnerability, sharing experiences

### MemoryStory (workshop)
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/workshop-memorystory/
- price: € 423,50
- duration: about 1 to 3 hours
- group: 4 to about 30
- audience: adults, pupils, students
- themes: personal development, resilience, growth, teamwork
- made with: Paper2Play

Pairs can also be "cause and effect" or "problem, feeling, solution", so it works for light topics and for sensitive ones as a conversation starter.

## Games (6 products, 4 games)

### The Elemental Five (3 products → one game with 3 versions in the new site)
- urls: https://innergames.nl/product/spel-the-elemental-five-compleet/ · https://innergames.nl/product/the-elemental-five-grondankers/ · https://innergames.nl/product/the-elemental-five-kaartenset/
- prices: **compleet € 152** · **grondankers € 79** · **kaartenset € 39**
- what's in each:
  - kaartenset: 36 large, sturdy cards (148 × 105 mm), the basis of the series. Play alone or in a group.
  - grondankers: 36 round floor cards (210 mm) for individual and group "opstellingen", to feel the elements with your body.
  - compleet: the card game + a set of grondankers + an extended digital manual + a PowerPoint for group workshops (manual and PowerPoint are also sold separately).
- the five elements: water, vuur, aarde, lucht, ether
- what it is: a series of games and workshops based on the Big Five personality model (the site says this itself). A Big Five test is accurate and well researched but very cognitive: it doesn't ask how something feels or where you want to grow. The Elemental Five adds that, through play, movement, imagination, opstellingen, breathing and meditation, and ends in clear points to work on. "Zonder hokjes" (no boxes). It's a system with many uses, from personal reflection to team coaching, and **very suitable for schools, for example for studieloopbaanbegeleiding (SLB)**.
- contact for the game, workshops and train-de-trainer: Ariën Kingma
- note: Damian's playable preview is in the separate `elemental-five` repo (on Vercel).

### Sociality, een kijk op sociale kwaliteit
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/sociality/
- price: € 69
- group: about 3 to 6 per set (use more sets for bigger groups)
- audience: people working or studying in social work, or anyone who wants to learn about Social Quality
- made by: the lectoraat Versterken van Sociale Kwaliteit of the HAN (Robin Smits, Nicole Moorman, Lisbeth Verharen, Carinda Jansen) with Ariën Kingma
- in the box: game box, board, 2 card sets of 21 cards with different cases, 1 set of 10 cards about social quality, a booklet with manual and theory, a pawn
- delivery: 2 to 3 working days in the Netherlands
- what it is: practice cases that teach you to use the Social Quality model in real situations, so the abstract theory becomes practical. More cases (micro, meso, macro) are being developed.

### Impact Imperium (klassenset)
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/impact-imperium-klassenset/
- price: € 275 (incl. shipping)
- in the set: 8 Impact Imperium games (for about 4 to 40 people), 1 conversation starter "Kansen(on)gelijkheid" (free), 10 challenge cards (for the losers), teacher manual and follow-up assignments, card sets, dice
- delivery: within 10 working days after confirmation
- made by: Duurzaamheid in de klas
- what it is: a very competitive game. Collect enough water, power and raw materials to make everyday products, without making too many harmful substances and plastic. Pupils or students learn how much it takes to make things, base decisions on it, and then turn it into concrete steps for their own behaviour.

### Kansen Keren (game)
- url: https://innergames.nl/product/kansen-keren/
- price: € 16,35 (incl. shipping in the Netherlands)
- made by: Carinda Jansen (buro KAN)
- what it is: a cooperative game about inequality of opportunity. **You can't win.** The group gives a pawn an identity, and it starts in the middle of a "levensweg". The dice decide which of 11 life moments happens (66 life events in total, at macro, meso and micro level). The group discusses each one and decides together how many steps the pawn moves up or down. Themes: living environment, health, social support, socio-economic status, relationships and sexual development, education, beliefs. The booklet has variations, for example 15 chairs instead of the board, with a participant as the pawn.

**Opleidingsbudgetten en subsidies:** aimed at organisations. Many organisations have training budgets, sector training schemes or subsidies for development, sustainable employability and cooperation, which can pay for (part of) a training. InnerGames offers to think along about what fits. The page lists schemes, for example:
- **SLIM-scholingssubsidie** (among others for SME employers, for training within recognised Ontwikkelpaden): up to € 25.000 per application, max € 100.000 per year per employer. Whether an InnerGames programme qualifies depends on the training and the Ontwikkelpad.
- **Expeditie** (partnerships of organisations and knowledge partners): research into innovative interventions for sustainable employability and lifelong learning.
- **FCB** (social work, youth care, childcare): professionalisation and development.
- more sectors further down the page (see the full file).

Why this matters for the redesign: HR people and schools often pay from a budget. A short line like "vaak te betalen uit een opleidingsbudget" plus a link could lower the barrier. Ask Damian before adding it.
